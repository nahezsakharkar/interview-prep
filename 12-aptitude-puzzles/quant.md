 Quant Fundamentals

Tags: #aptitude #math
Difficulty: Medium
Status: Learning

## Definition

Quant fundamentals cover arithmetic reasoning, ratio and proportion, percentage, probability, and time-based word problems.

## Why it matters / when to use

They are common in aptitude rounds and quick interviews that test reasoning speed and accuracy.

## How it works

Break down the relation between known and unknown quantities and simplify carefully before calculating.

## Code example

```ts
const percent = (value: number, total: number) => (value / total) * 100;
console.log(percent(30, 120)); // 25
```

## Time and space complexity

Not usually relevant; focus is on logical clarity and arithmetic accuracy.

## Common mistakes and pitfalls

- Missing units or conversions
- Misreading the base in percentage problems
- Rushing without checking the final answer for plausibility

## Interview questions

### Q: How do you approach a ratio problem?
Model answer: Convert the ratio into a common multiplier and map it to the real quantities before solving.

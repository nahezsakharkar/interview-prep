 12 - Aptitude & Puzzles

This section focuses on rational reasoning, quick calculations, and classic interview puzzles that test problem-solving under constraints.

## Notes

- [Quant fundamentals](quant.md) — percentages, ratios, averages, time-speed-distance, probability basics
- [Logical reasoning](logical-reasoning.md) — data interpretation, syllogisms, work and calendar patterns
- [Classic puzzles](puzzles.md) — river crossing, coin problems, clock and probability puzzles

## Interview approach

- Think aloud when possible.
- Start with a small concrete example.
- Separate observation from assumption.
- Verify the final answer before concluding.

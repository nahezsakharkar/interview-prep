 Common HR Questions

Tags: #behavioral #hr
Difficulty: Easy
Status: Learning

## Definition

These are standard interview questions about teamwork, conflict, motivation, failure, and career growth.

## Why it matters / when to use

They help the interviewer assess judgment, communication, and self-awareness.

## How it works

Use a brief structure: context, what you did, result, what you learned.

## Code example

```md
Question: Tell me about a time you failed.
Answer: I underestimated integration risk in a release and missed one dependency check.
```

## Time and space complexity

Not applicable.

## Common mistakes and pitfalls

- Framing failure as incompetence instead of learning
- Making all stories about technical wins only
- Failing to quantify impact

## Interview questions

### Q: How do you answer "Why do you want to leave your current role?"
Model answer: Be honest, but keep it focused on growth, challenge, and alignment with the opportunity rather than criticism of your current company.

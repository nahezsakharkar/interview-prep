# Salary negotiation

## Core idea

Negotiation is about aligning compensation with market value and the scope of the work. Do not frame it as a personal demand; frame it as a business alignment conversation.

## Useful preparation

- Research market ranges for the same role and geography
- Understand the company band or role level
- Evaluate your value based on skills and scope

## Good answer pattern

> Based on the role scope and market data, I am targeting a range of X to Y. I am excited about the role, and I wanted to align compensation with the responsibilities and impact this position has.

## Common mistakes

- Showing a number before learning the role context
- Negotiating on base compensation alone
- Making it emotional instead of data-driven

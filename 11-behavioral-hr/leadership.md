# Leadership examples

## What interviewers look for

- ownership
- communication and clarity
- decision-making under uncertainty
- influence without formal authority

## Good story structure

- Context: what was happening?
- Problem: what was at stake?
- Action: what did you do?
- Result: what changed?

## Example

```md
Situation: The team was blocked by unclear ownership on a cross-functional feature.
Task: I needed to unblock delivery without creating friction.
Action: I aligned the stakeholders, clarified the decision owner, and simplified the rollout.
Result: We shipped in time and reduced rework in the next sprint.
```

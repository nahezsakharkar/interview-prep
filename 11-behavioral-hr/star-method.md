# STAR method

## Definition

STAR is a simple structure for behavioral answers: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

## How to use it

### Situation
Describe the context briefly.

### Task
State what you needed to deliver or solve.

### Action
Explain what you actually did, emphasizing judgment and ownership.

### Result
Cite measurable outcome or clear learning and impact.

## Example

```md
Situation: We had a release with flaky deployment validation.
Task: I had to reduce failed deployments without slowing delivery.
Action: I introduced a smoke test gate, clarified ownership, and added rollback checks.
Result: Failed deployments dropped by 35% in two months.
```

## Good interview habits

- Keep it brief: 60-120 seconds per story
- Emphasize your contribution, not only team effort
- Use metrics whenever available
- Tie the story to the job requirement

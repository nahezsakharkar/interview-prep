 Behavioral Story: <Title>

## STAR

### Situation

Describe the context, team, challenge, and timing. Keep it concrete and brief.

### Task

Explain your responsibility and the outcome you were expected to achieve.

### Action

Describe what you did, with emphasis on decision-making, trade-offs, and collaboration.

### Result

Quantify the impact when possible: performance, delivery, quality, revenue, or adoption.

## What it demonstrates

- Ownership
- Problem solving
- Communication
- Leadership
- Learning mindset

## Follow-up questions

- What was the hardest decision you had to make?
- What would you do differently today?
- How did this affect the broader team or product?
